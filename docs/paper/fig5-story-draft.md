# Proximal tubule programs are staged differently in mouse and human

We compared positional expression profiles for 3,912 one-to-one orthologs along the proximal tubule (PT) in two mouse kidneys and two cortical sections from one human donor (Fig. 5a). Of these genes, 278 varied at least twofold along the PT in both sections of either group. This included 195 genes that met the cutoff only in mouse, 30 only in human and 53 in both. Clustering the full human–mouse contrast profiles revealed differences in both gradient strength and where along the PT expression changed (Fig. 5b).

In mouse, genes with graded PT expression switched in two waves, near positions 0.36–0.37 and 0.65–0.68 on the shared coordinate. Among 93 genes with classifiable switches in the human sections, 25 early-switching mouse genes switched closer to 0.65 in human, and 38 late-switching genes switched near 0.63. Seven genes, including *SLC22A12* and *SLC5A10*, retained early switches in both groups (Fig. 5c). Scoring the early and late programs in individual tubules recapitulated this organization without using position to order the tubules. Mouse tubules passed through a state with a high early-program score and a low late-program score. The two scores rose together in the human sections (Fig. 5d).

The intervening mouse state had a distinct transcriptional composition. Of 129 mouse S2 genes, 40 declined on entry to S2, including the amino-acid transporters *Slc6a19* and *Slc7a9*. Forty-eight rose, including the fatty-acid oxidation genes *Ehhadh* and *Acadl*. A further 34 peaked within S2, including the amino-acid catabolism genes *Cndp2*, *Aldh6a1* and *Aass*, and seven reached a trough there. The same heatmap rows show much weaker separation of these programs in the sampled human mid-PT (Fig. 5e).

We then asked where positional structure was stronger in human. Of 113 orthologs with stronger human gradients, 44 fell into two early-weighted groups. Eleven of these genes were lysosomal, including *CTSB*, *CTSF*, *CTSH*, *LGMN*, *TPP1* and *LAMP1*. Across all 57 screened lysosomal genes, 41 had a larger early-minus-late contrast in the observed human profiles (Fig. 5f,g). At the other end of the PT, *GPT*, *DCXR*, *GGACT*, *PTER* and *ABHD14A* rose together in the human profiles. *GPT* showed a modest middle peak in mouse and rose sharply toward the end in human, while *GPT2* remained early-weighted in both groups (Fig. 5h). In this human kidney, selected early and late programs thus had sharper positional profiles alongside a less distinct mouse-like intermediate program.

## Figure legend

**Fig. 5 | Positional organization of mouse and human proximal tubule programs.** **a,** All 3,912 screened one-to-one orthologs, ordered by mouse positional bias. Heatmaps show gene-centered fitted log₂(CP10k + 0.5), averaged equally across sections within each group. **b,** The 278 genes with a fitted twofold range in both sections of either group. Sidebars denote four clusters of standardized human-minus-mouse contrast curves and give their sizes. **c,** Paired switch positions for 93 classifiable genes. Each point is one gene. Positions average two sections per group. **d,** Early- and late-program scores from 11,984 tubules. Each line shows 20 equal-count bins ordered by the mean of the two scores within one section. Solid and dashed lines distinguish the two sections. **e,** The 129 mouse S2 genes, grouped by expression change in S2. Dashed lines mark the approximate mouse S2 boundaries. **f,** The 113 genes whose pooled human log₂ range was at least 0.5, at least 0.25 above mouse and at least 1.65 times the mouse range with a denominator floor of 0.1. Rows are grouped by standardized human profile shape. **g,** Early-minus-late observed log₂ rates for 57 lysosomal genes. Ochre marks the 11 in the early-weighted groups in **f**. **h,** Centered *GPT* and *GPT2* fitted profiles and observed position-bin means. The comparison uses two mouse kidneys and two cortical sections from one human donor.
