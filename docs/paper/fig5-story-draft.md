# Proximal tubule programs are staged differently in mouse and human

We compared positional expression profiles for 3,912 one-to-one orthologs along the proximal tubule (PT) in two mouse kidneys and two cortical sections from one human donor. Of these genes, 278 varied at least twofold along the PT in both sections of either group. This included 195 genes that met the cutoff only in mouse, 30 only in human and 53 in both (Fig. 5a).

In mouse, genes with graded PT expression switched in two waves, near positions 0.36–0.37 and 0.65–0.68 on the shared coordinate. Among 93 genes with classifiable switches in the human sections, 25 early-switching mouse genes switched closer to 0.65 in human, and 38 late-switching genes switched near 0.63. Seven genes, including *SLC22A12* and *SLC5A10*, retained early switches in both groups (Fig. 5b). Between the two mouse waves, the fraction of tubules with a positive early-program score and a nonpositive late-program score reached 84% and 94% in the two mice and 25% and 22% in the two human sections (Fig. 5c). When the two scores were compared without the positional coordinate, they rose in sequence in mouse and approximately together in the human sections.

The intervening mouse state had a distinct transcriptional composition. Of 129 mouse S2 genes, 40 declined on entry to S2, including the amino-acid transporters *Slc6a19* and *Slc7a9*. Forty-eight rose, including the fatty-acid oxidation genes *Ehhadh* and *Acadl*. A further 34 peaked within S2, including the amino-acid catabolism genes *Cndp2*, *Aldh6a1* and *Aass*, and seven reached a trough there. The decline, peak and rise that defined this intermediate state in mouse were much less distinct in the sampled human mid-PT (Fig. 5d,e).

We then asked where positional structure was stronger in human. Of 113 orthologs with stronger human gradients, 44 fell into two early-weighted groups. Eleven of these genes were lysosomal, including *CTSB*, *CTSF*, *CTSH*, *LGMN*, *TPP1* and *LAMP1*. Across all 57 screened lysosomal genes, 41 had a larger early-minus-late contrast in the observed human profiles, including stronger early bias across the 11 highlighted genes (Fig. 5f,g). At the other end of the PT, *GPT*, *DCXR*, *GGACT*, *PTER* and *ABHD14A* rose together in the human profiles (Fig. 5h). *GPT* showed a modest middle peak in mouse and rose sharply toward the end in human, while *GPT2* remained early-weighted in both groups (Fig. 5i). In this human kidney, selected early and late programs thus had sharper positional profiles alongside a less distinct mouse-like intermediate program.

## Figure precedents

The main comparison puts transition timing on one coordinate, following Bendall et al.'s trajectory profiles and coordination windows. The S2 heatmaps sit beside program means, and the last row uses mean profiles and named genes to link an overall change to a biological example. Those choices follow the progression from cluster shape to functional examples in Moor et al. and the comparable mouse and human module profiles in Zwick et al. [Bendall et al., Figs. 2 and 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4045247/) · [Moor et al., Figs. 3 and 5](https://shalevlab.weizmann.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Moor_2018.pdf) · [Zwick et al., Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11654995/)
